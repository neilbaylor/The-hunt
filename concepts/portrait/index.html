Portrait HUD board

Twelve directions for playing on a phone held upright. In each one the camera sits behind your trail shoulder, so you fill the lower half of the screen, and the HUD stays out of your way. Every direction has the same controls: club, swing mode, lie with slope, the swing stick, and the spin ball while the ball is in the air. Directions marked Live have a working stick: pull down, wait for the flash, push up.

Swing stick lab

Eight ways to make the stick obvious

All of these are live. Each one measures the same things: how far you pull, how straight you pull, when you push, and the push angle. Pick the one that feels best in your hand, and any direction above can use it.

What every layout keeps

You stay in shotThe camera is behind your trail shoulder. You take up the lower-left half, and the range and target fill the sky above you.
One thumbThe stick sits where your thumb rests, bottom right by default (it can mirror). It is 140–200 pt tall, so a full pull is a real gesture.
Top-of-swing flashWhen your golfer reaches the top, the stick glows. Push up then. Early reads Quick and late reads Late.
Spin replaces the stickOnce the ball is in the air, the stick's spot turns into the spin ball. Swipe down for backspin, up for topspin, sideways to bend it.
Backgrounds are real renders from the game engine (the golfer model is still being upgraded). The panels are HTML, so every layout can be lifted straight into the game.
