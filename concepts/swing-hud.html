Swing & HUD concepts

A playable range with the pull-back, push-through swing pad, the club, lie and swing-mode buttons, and the spin ball you swipe while the ball is in the air. Switch between three looks for the HUD. The range is a quick 2D stand-in for the real 3D scene.

Hit some balls

Swipe the ball to add spin
1 · Pull backPress the ball on the swing pad and pull straight down. The further you pull, the longer the shot. Drifting sideways costs accuracy.
2 · Feel the topThe golfer turns at their own pace. The pad ring flashes when they reach the top. Start pushing up right then.
3 · Push throughPush straight up, quickly. Angle it left for a draw (more is a hook), right for a fade or slice.
4 · Add spinWhile the ball flies, swipe the spin ball. Down is backspin, up is topspin, sideways bends it.

Tap the club, swing or lie panels to change them. Tapping the lie card cycles through sample lies so you can see the slope arrow move the ball. Best in landscape on a phone, or use full screen.

Three looks

Same parts in each: club button, swing mode, lie card with the slope arrow, the swing pad, and the spin ball. Pick one, or mix them (say, the Clubhouse pad with Broadcast panels).

Each golfer will later get their own tempo (how fast they reach the top), so a quick swinger and a slow swinger feel different on the same pad. That is where your friends' swing styles plug in.
